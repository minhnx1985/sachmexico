## Product

Create a Vietnamese landing page with the editorial concept:

# **MEXICO QUA NHỮNG TRANG SÁCH**
## **Năm tác giả, nhiều cánh cửa bước vào văn chương Mexico**

The page presents Mexican literature already introduced — and soon to be introduced — in Vietnamese by Nhã Nam.

Core authors:
1. Juan Villoro
2. Octavio Paz
3. Alberto Ruy-Sánchez
4. Carlos Fuentes
5. Cristina Rivera Garza

## Why this page exists now

The page is timed to the **Lễ hội Văn hóa Thế giới tại Hà Nội 2026**, 01–04/10/2026.
It should feel culturally timely, not like a dormant backlist archive.

Cristina Rivera Garza is the “what comes next” spotlight.

## User goals

Within seconds, a visitor should understand:
- this is about Mexico through literature, not tourist symbolism;
- the five authors are radically different;
- which author/book is a good entry point;
- which books are available/current at Nhã Nam;
- which works are part of Nhã Nam's historical Mexico catalogue;
- what Vietnamese press has written about Mexican literature.

## Primary interaction goals
- explore author modules;
- open book lightboxes/details;
- click to active Nhã Nam product pages where valid;
- explore the press library;
- discover the forthcoming Cristina Rivera Garza title.
